01 — The practice

Where precision meets possibility.

PTC (Parvati Techno Consultants) is a well-established professional structural design consultancy firm based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. PTC is a trusted name in the field of structural design and allied services, offering integrated consultancy in a wide range of civil, architectural, structural and PEB projects.

The firm was started in the '90s as a structural engineering consultancy by Mr. J. Satapara. With over 35 years of professional service and numerous satisfied clients, PTC is a trusted name in civil, structural, industrial planning and design. 1,250+ successfully completed projects across India display clients' trust in our services.

Mr. Hrushikesh Patel is presently heading the company as owner and principal consultant. We collaborate with architects, developers, MNCs, industrialists, EPC contractors, PEB vendors and government organisations to realise their vision and requirements.

01

Pre-design & planning

Industrial planning, feasibility of concepts, health assessment of existing structures, quantity survey & estimation, and pre-bid engineering for EPC contractors.

02

Design & documentation

Architectural and structural engineering design, MEPF, HVAC and interiors with associates, proof checking, peer review, design audit and tender preparation.

03

Procurement & detailing

Shop and fabrication drawings, bar-bending schedules, independent cost audits, value engineering, and bill checking & certifying.

04

Construction support & audit

Structural auditing, strengthening and retrofitting, and design resolution on site through construction until handover.
